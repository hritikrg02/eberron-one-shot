A **Minor Dragonmark** is the smallest and simplest of all [[Dragonmark\|Dragonmarks]]. It is the most common among those with [[Dragonmark\|Dragonmarks]], though is still exceedingly rare among the general populous. It allows the user to use their [[Dragonmark\|Dragonmark]]’s abilities, though not to the power level of someone with a [[Greater Dragonmark\|Greater Dragonmark]] or [[Siberys Dragonmark\|Siberys Dragonmark]]. It is usually no larger than the size of someone’s palm.
