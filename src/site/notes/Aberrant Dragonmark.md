An **Aberrant Dragonmark** appearing on someone is typically the result of two individuals with different [[Dragonmark\|Dragonmarks]] having a child, though this mark can simply manifest on an individual as well (as is the case with all [[Dragonmark\|Dragonmarks]]). The powers granted by this [[Dragonmark\|Dragonmark]] differ from person to person, but generally grant nontrivial power to those who possess it though comes at some cost (also varying from person to person).

Very few in [[Khorvaire\|Khorvaire]] possess Aberrant Dragonmarks. This is primarily due to two things: 1) individuals possessing two different dragonmarks are strictly forbidden from having children (often meaning having anything beyond a platonic relationship being considered taboo) and 2) the near eradication of all those who posses Aberrant Dragonmarks during and following the [[War of the Mark\|War of the Mark]]. That being said, there has been a notable uptick in their presence following [[The Day of Mourning\|The Day of Mourning]].
