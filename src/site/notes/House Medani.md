> [!quote] Baron Trelib d’Medani
> If you want a wall of muscle to get between you and a blade, go to [[House Deneith\|House Deneith]]. If you want someone to anticipate the threat and make sure you aren’t event in the room with whoever’s holding the blade, that’s what we do.

**House Medani** is one of [[The Dragonmarked Houses\|The Dragonmarked Houses]] and controls the [[Mark of Detection\|Mark of Detection]]. They are intricately familiar with the world of subterfuge – not engaging in it, but being aware of it’s existence and doing something with that information. They are also use this ability in their mercantile efforts, such that they may ascertain the quality of goods. They also operate the [[Warning Guild\|Warning Guild]].
