**House Phiarlan** is one of [[The Dragonmarked Houses\|The Dragonmarked Houses]] and is one of two houses that control the [[Mark of Shadow\|Mark of Shadow]], the other being [[House Thuranni\|House Thuranni]]. This separation was the result of the [[Shadow Schism\|Shadow Schism]]. House Philarlan uses the [[Mark of Shadow\|Mark of Shadow]] differently from [[House Thuranni\|House Thuranni]] in that, although many spies fill their ranks, operations are usually less covert with many going into entertainment instead, using their abilities to watch those with the coin to afford such libations.
