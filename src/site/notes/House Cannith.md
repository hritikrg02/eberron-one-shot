> [!quote] Barron Merrix d’Cannith
> My house built the modern world. [[House Orien\|Orien]] may drive the [[Lightning Rail\|Lightning Rail]], but it’s Cannith who builds the cars and lays the stones it travels on. Cannith makes the ever-bright lanterns hold the night at bay. Smith, carpenter, alchemist – the best all carry my seal.

**House Cannith** is one of [[The Dragonmarked Houses\|The Dragonmarked Houses]] and controls the [[Mark of Making\|Mark of Making]]. Formerly operating within [[Cyre\|Cyre]], this house is responsible for the creation of the [[Warforged\|Warforged]]. Most forms of manufacturing and construction in [[Khorvaire\|Khorvaire]] can be traced back to this house, whether that be house members or those who study at institutions run by them.

With [[The Day of Mourning\|The Day of Mourning]] and subsequent destruction of [[Cyre\|Cyre]], the house lies somewhat fractured as there are now three leaders all vying for house control.
