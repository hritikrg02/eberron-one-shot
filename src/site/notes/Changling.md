**Changlings** are beings that originate from the [[Feywild\|Feywild]] but have since made their way to [[Eberron\|Eberron]] and by extension, [[Khorvaire\|Khorvaire]]. In their base form, they appear almost devoid of any features, with their skin appearing greyish in color. However, they have the ability to appear as any other species in all of [[Eberron\|Eberron]], though there are a few albeit minor caveats. A changling needs to have seen the person they wish to appear as at least once before they are able to do so, they cannot change their physical size (i.e. they cannot appear much larger or smaller than their original form), and they must have the same arrangement of limbs (i.e. they are humanoid). Each of the forms a changling takes is referred to as a [[Persona\|Persona]]. They excel at social encounters for this reason.

In changling culture, it is commonplace for [[Persona\|Persona]]s to be shared among many changlings. In fact, [[Persona\|Persona]]s are oft handed down in a family, allowing the child of a changling parent to take advantage of the relationships already forged by their parent’s [[Persona\|Persona]].

In [[Eberron\|Eberron]], changlings are often discriminated against due to fear that one may take the form of someone one knows. To that end, there are three main schools of thought within changling culture:
- [[Passers\|Passers]]
- [[Becomers\|Becomers]]
- [[Reality Seekers\|Reality Seekers]]
