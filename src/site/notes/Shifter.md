**Shifters** are the descendants of those who originally suffered from [[Lycanthopy\|Lycanthopy]]. As shifters gave birth to more shifters, the humanoid and animal forms began to merge somewhat, resulting in the shifters of today. Unlike those suffering from the curse, shifters always appear animal-esque though with a distinctly humanoid appearance. When they do shift (something they are able to do at will) their animalistic traits are enhanced, such as their claws or teeth increasing in length.
