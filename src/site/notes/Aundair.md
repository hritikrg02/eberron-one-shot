The nation of **Aundair** was one of [[The Five Nations\|The Five Nations]] under [[The Kingdom of Galifar\|The Kingdom of Galifar]]. In the modern age, it’s best known for its pleasant summers and short winters, leading to it being the farming capital of [[Eberron\|Eberron]], with many rural populations spread throughout the land. Moreover, Aundair is also known for it’s abundant use of magic in many parts of life. This is most prevalent in the floating city of [[Arcanix\|Arcanix]] and the capital of [[Fairhaven\|Fairhaven]].

Life in Aundair is typically seen as pleasant and homey, with many living in the countryside. In the last few decades however, the rural population of Aundair has been steadily declining, as more and more people decide to move into cities.
