> [!quote] Kalaash’arna, Tharashk inquisitive
> Are we the prey? No, we are the hunters!

**Orcs**, like [[Goblin\|Goblins]], are native to [[Khorvaire\|Khorvaire]] and are known for being extremely in-touch with the nature of the realm, with many being the first [[Druid\|Druids]] of [[Eberron\|Eberron]]. 

They are the only species that have successfully managed to settle in the [[Shadow Marches\|Shadow Marches]] of [[Khorvaire\|Khorvaire]], and have a thriving civilization contained therein. They were also the species that managed to stop the [[Daelkyr Invasion\|Daelkyr Invasion]] of the [[Shadow Marches\|Shadow Marches]]. In that same vein, orcs are currently attempting to fend of the [[Demon\|Demons]] of the [[Demon Wastes\|Demon Wastes]] in order to settle there as well.
