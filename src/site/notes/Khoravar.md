**Khoravar** are the descendants of both [[Human\|Humans]] and [[Elf\|Elves]], though they find the term “half-elf” offensive. The term “Khoravar” itself is an [[Elvish\|Elvish]] word meaning “children of [[Khorvaire\|Khorvaire]]” and they consider themselves as such. When first appearing on the continent, most were forced out of any [[Elf\|Elven]] nation or settlement. Faced with that, many chose to not consider themselves as a mix between two peoples’ but rather as the children of the continent itself, as they see themselves as from [[Khorvaire\|Khorvaire]]. 

As such, Khoravar have positioned themselves as the best at navigating both cultures simultaneously, acting as a bridge. Many hold positions as [[Bards\|Bards]], [[Diplomats\|Diplomats]], [[Mediators\|Mediators]], or [[Translators\|Translators]]. Some however, wish to reconnect with their [[Feywild\|Feywild]] ancestry, becoming [[Druids\|Druids]] or [[Warlocks\|Warlocks]].
