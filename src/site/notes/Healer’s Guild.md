The **Healer’s Guild** is operated and staffed by [[House Jorasco\|House Jorasco]]. They offer healthcare to anyone who is willing to pay – the greater the service, the higher the cost. If one is unable to pay for the highest quality of treatment (often resulting in complete restoration), more mundane and non-magical techniques are used instead.
