> [!quote] Bali d’Orien, veteran courier
> We get things where they need to go. It doesn’t matter if it’s a letter, a person, or a hundred tons of steel. Whether we have to cross mountains, rivers, or [[The Mournlands\|The Mournlands]] itself, Orien finds a way.

**House Orien** is one of [[The Dragonmarked Houses\|The Dragonmarked Houses]] and controls the [[Mark of Passage\|Mark of Passage]]. They are responsible for the flow of nearly everything throughout all of [[Khorvaire\|Khorvaire]] and have been for centuries. They operate both the [[Lightning Rail\|Lightning Rail]] and the [[Courier’s Guild\|Courier’s Guild]], in addition to smaller operations of caravans and coaches.
