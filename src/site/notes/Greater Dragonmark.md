A **Greater Dragonmark** is a more developed and powerful version of a [[Minor Dragonmark\|Minor Dragonmark]]. They only manifest on those who already possess a [[Minor Dragonmark\|Minor Dragonmark]] and when manifested, expand outwards from the original mark becoming two to three times the original size. Usage of one’s [[Minor Dragonmark\|Minor Dragonmark]] can sometimes lead to Greater Dragonmarks being formed on the bearer’s body. Due to their increased power, those with these marks are incredibly important to [[The Dragonmarked Houses\|The Dragonmarked Houses]] in nearly every way and are treated as such.
