> [!quote] Mror proverb
> Gold is the gift of the mountains, but iron comes from blood and bone.

**Dwaves**, like [[Elf\|Elves]], are not native to [[Khorvaire\|Khorvaire]] but come from [[Frostfell\|Frostfell]]. They largely settled in what is now known as [[Mror Holds\|Mror Holds]] and largely remained there until the arrival of [[Humans\|Humans]]. Dwarves have since been known to be found all across [[Khorvaire\|Khorvaire]] society. 

They have a propensity to seek resources and extract value therein, having an extreme capitalistic sense in [[Eberron\|Eberron]]. Many of the richest families in [[Eberron\|Eberron]] are dwarves, with professions such as bankers and coal barrons being commonplace.
