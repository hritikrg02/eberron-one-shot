**Becomers** refer to a cultural group within [[Changling\|Changling]] society defined by their liberal use of their ability to have multiple [[Persona\|Persona]]s and using that ability to their advantage. Becomers are not inherently evil or malicious, but rather see their ability to shift as their nature and wish to embrace it.
