Compared to the almost idyllic plains of [[Aundair\|Aundair]], **Breland** is a nation of industry and progress. It was one of the [[The Five Nations\|The Five Nations]] under [[The Kingdom of Galifar\|The Kingdom of Galifar]]. [[Brelanders\|Brelanders]] value individuality and independence above all else, often struggling to work as a group. Despite this, many other nations fear Breland as one of, if not the most powerful nation in all of [[Khorvaire\|Khorvaire]]. This is due in no small part to the largest city in [[Eberron\|Eberron]], [[Sharn, the City of Towers\|Sharn]].
