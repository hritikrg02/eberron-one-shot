**Kalashtar** are a race of people within [[Eberron\|Eberron]] originally born from the psionic union of monks who took in dream spirits (known as the [[Quori\|Quori]]) into their bodies and allowed two souls to occupy a single body. As these original kalashtar would go on to have children of their own, the [[Quori\|Quori]] spirit occupying someone would get passed down to their children, though the presence would be divided among all children. 

After many, many generations, the original spirits have become so divided that modern kalashtar are no longer able to communicate with their spirit but are still able to sense them. This manifests as being able to tap into the spirits’ memories, providing a source of inspiration/instinct, granting minor psionic abilities, and defense from psionic attacks. Regardless, many kalashtar struggle with their identity, since their mind is both theirs and some number of others simultaneously.
