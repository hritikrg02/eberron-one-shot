> What’s the price of a life? Well, I’ve got a rate sheet over here. I’d be happy to discuss it.

**House Jorasco** is one of [[The Dragonmarked Houses\|The Dragonmarked Houses]] and controls the [[Mark of Healing\|Mark of Healing]]. They operate the [[Healer’s Guild\|Healer’s Guild]] and as a result of their relentless push to seek out those with the [[Mark of Healing\|Mark of Healing]] not already part of the house, operate a near-monopoly on healing.
