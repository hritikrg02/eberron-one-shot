> [!quote] Rotting Bal, Tarkanan enforcer

Unlike the rest of [[The Dragonmarked Houses\|The Dragonmarked Houses]], **House Tarkanan** is not a house in the traditional sense. They act much more as a clandestine set of operatives engaging in organized crime throughout [[Khorvaire\|Khorvaire]].
