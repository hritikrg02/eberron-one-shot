The nation of **Karrnath** was one of the [[The Five Nations\|The Five Nations]] in [[The Kingdom of Galifar\|The Kingdom of Galifar]]. The region is subject to harsh winters and storms. Although known primarily for their martial might (with many convinced that the nation would have won [[The Last War\|The Last War]] had it continued), Karrnath is also known for its liberal use of necromancy, a magical form either looked down upon or outright illegal in other nations. Much of the architecture in Karrnath follows that of a gothic style.

Owing to their military lifestyle, all [[Karrns\|Karrns]] are mandated to perform military service. Once, when they come of age, and again once they have died, as an undead. This service is not hated by [[Karrns\|Karrns]] – in fact many welcome the stronger military force that this allows. As a result, the Karrnath military is extremely sizable and formidable, and filled with soldiers that do not require to eat or sleep.

Being so far up north and unable to farm, food is often a scarcity within Karrnath while disease has a tendency to run rampant. As such, food, clothing, and other essentials are imported from other nations, making them heavily reliant on other countries instead of being self-sufficient.

For a period of time, the national religion of Karrnath was [[The Blood of Vol\|The Blood of Vol]]. Although no longer the case, the religion is still practiced openly.
