A **Siberys Dragonmark** is the greatest known evolution of a [[Dragonmark\|Dragonmark]] and grant the bearer immense power. Why they manifest is a complete mystery, as it has been known that even those who practice with diligence may never obtain one while some unmarked scion of a [[The Dragonmarked Houses\|House]] may suddenly display one against all expectations. 

People who manifest these marks (known as an [[Heir of Siberys\|Heir of Siberys]]) are known to be targets of assassination if their abilities are widely known or if their actions do not align with that of their mark’s corresponding house.
