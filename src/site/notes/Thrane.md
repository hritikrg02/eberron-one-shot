One of [[The Five Nations\|The Five Nations]] of [[The Kingdom of Galifar\|The Kingdom of Galifar]], **Thrane** is currently a theocratic state within [[Khorvaire\|Khorvaire]] ruled by the [[Church of the Silver Flame\|Church of the Silver Flame]]. Thrane, much like [[Aundair\|Aundair]], is known for it’s magic. However unlike [[Aundair\|Aundair]], this magic is not arcane in nature – rather it is divine. 

It is believed by other nations that Thrane is nation of religions zealots where dissenters are oppressed. Thrane is quite isolationist in its policy, often left out of talks between other nations. Almost no nation has any trade relations with Thrane, and as such its citizens are taxed immensely and shortages on food are commonplace.
