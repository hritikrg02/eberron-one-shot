To date, several different myths exist that describe the **Creation of Eberron**. However, there are some key elements that are common between them all.

At the dawn of time, three [[Progenitor Dragon\|Progenitor Dragon]]s would come into being: [[Siberys (Progenitor Dragon)\|Siberys]], [[Eberron (Progenitor Dragon)\|Eberron]], and [[Khyber (Progenitor Dragon)\|Khyber]]. Through their power would thirteen planes of existence be formed, each embodying a concept. The fourteenth and final work would be the [[Eberron\|Material Plane]]. But before it could be brought to fruition, cruel [[Khyber (Progenitor Dragon)\|Khyber]] sought dominion over their creation and with her might attacked [[Siberys (Progenitor Dragon)\|Siberys]] and killed him in cold blood. Knowing that she did not have the power to kill her outright, [[Eberron (Progenitor Dragon)\|Eberron]] intervened and restrained [[Khyber (Progenitor Dragon)\|Khyber]] with her tail – not defeating her, but imprisoning her. These cosmic beings would then become part of the world itself:
- [[Siberys (Progenitor Dragon)\|Siberys]], the [[Siberys\|world above]],
- [[Eberron\|Eberron]], the [[Eberron\|Material Plane]], and
- [[Khyber (Progenitor Dragon)\|Khyber]], the [[Khyber\|world below]]

Variations on the above legend can be found throughout history. In one text, for example, it is said that [[Eberron (Progenitor Dragon)\|Eberron]] did not restrain [[Khyber (Progenitor Dragon)\|Khyber]] with her tail at all for she was not strong enough to do so. Instead, she lay on top of her with [[Siberys (Progenitor Dragon)\|Siberys]] floating above them both, leading to the layering of the world as we see it today.
