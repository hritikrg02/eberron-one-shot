The **Banking Guild** is a group staffed and operated by [[House Kundarak\|House Kundarak]] that runs nearly every major financial institution in [[Khorvaire\|Khorvaire]] including banks and stock exchanges. The coin of anything – from individuals to major corporations – as been known to flow through their coffers.
