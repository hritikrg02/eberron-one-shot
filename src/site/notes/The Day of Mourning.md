On 20 Olarune 994 YK, an event that would become known as **The Day of Mourning** would occur. On this day, a magical blast erupted completed destroyed the nation of [[Cyre\|Cyre]]. The entire land was laid to waste and would become known as [[The Mournlands\|The Mournlands]]. Two out of every three [[Cryan\|Cryan]]s were killed in the blast. It is widely believed that the reigning monarch at the time, [[Queen Dannel ir’Wynarn\|Queen Dannel ir’Wynarn]], was killed in the blast, seeing as she was within the nation’s capital, [[Metrol\|Metrol]]. The only survivors were those who lived on the border of other nations, who quickly fled (see [[Cyran Exodus\|Cyran Exodus]]). To many, this and the [[The Last War\|The Last War]] represent the death of possibility within [[Eberron\|Eberron]].

To this day, the cause of the Day of Mourning is completely unknown. Fear of the event occurring a second time is what brought the leaders of all nations together to write and sign [[The Treaty of Thronehold\|The Treaty of Thronehold]], ending [[The Last War\|The Last War]].
