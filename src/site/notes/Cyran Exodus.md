The **Cyran Exodus** was an event that followed [[The Day of Mourning\|The Day of Mourning]], where [[Cyran\|Cyran]]s who managed to avoid being killed by the magical blast due to residing near the border of another nation, quickly fled the region and attempted to seek refuge elsewhere. [[Aundair\|Aundair]], the only allied nation to [[Cyre\|Cyre]], turned away the refugees, fearful that allowing them within its borders would bring disaster upon them. The [[Valenar\|elves to the south]], enlisted by [[Cyre\|Cyre]] to fight on their behalf, slaughtered any all refugees that entered their borders. The [[Goblin\|Goblin]]s of [[Darguun\|Darguun]] did the same. When questioned as to why this bloodshed needed occur, both said the people of [[Cyre\|Cyre]] who were fleeing were, “invading their lands”.

As a result, of the one and three survivors of [[The Day of Mourning\|The Day of Mourning]], many, if not most, perished in the weeks and months that followed. Those who survived can now be found all over [[Khorvaire\|Khorvaire]].
