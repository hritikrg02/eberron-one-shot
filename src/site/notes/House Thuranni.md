> [!quote] Lady Elara d’Thuranni, shadow dancer
> The illusions that I weave dazzle and deceive. Sometimes that deception eases your burdens, letting you forget your troubles for a moment. But I can also ease your burdens by ending your life.

**House Thuranni** is one of [[The Dragonmarked Houses\|The Dragonmarked Houses]] and is one of two houses that control the [[Mark of Shadow\|Mark of Shadow]], the other being [[House Phiarlan\|House Phiarlan]]. This separation was the result of the [[Shadow Schism\|Shadow Schism]]. House Thuranni uses the [[Mark of Shadow\|Mark of Shadow]] for espionage, with many members being covert spies, assassins, and infiltrators.
