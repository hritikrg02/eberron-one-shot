This page is a meta-document that explains what this site is and how it’s meant to be used. This “encyclopedia” is a wiki-style set of rendered text files, meant to provide information on my version of the [[Eberron\|Eberron]] campaign setting. In essence, it’s the standard campaign setting alongside anything that my players and I come up with. For a bit of flair and fun, it’s written in-universe by chronicler [[Arthur Buell\|Arthur Buell]]. 

The purpose of this wiki is twofold. The first is for me (the DM) to have a working copy of everything that makes up the world. It allows me to world-build faster, more precisely, and with more frequency that I otherwise would have. 

The second is for my players. This wiki is meant to be a quick reference that they can use to look up information about the world without needing to scour the internet or have every single thing written down. That does not mean this wiki can serve in place of note-taking, but it can work as a reference for specific people, places, or things as they become known to the players.

That concludes the introduction. I would encourage anyone reading to head back to the [[001 Administrative/Welcome\|Welcome]] page and explore from there!
