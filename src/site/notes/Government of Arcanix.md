The **Government of Arcanix**, though technically a monarchy, can be more accurately be described as governed by a council. Members of this council include university officials such as deans and headmasters, as well as the leaders of the [[Knights Arcane\|Knights Arcane]], the primary military force of [[Aundair\|Aundair]].
