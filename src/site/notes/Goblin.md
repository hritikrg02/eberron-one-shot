**Goblins** are native to the continent of [[Khorvaire\|Khorvaire]] and once had an empire that stretched the continent known as the [[Empire of Dhakaan\|Empire of Dhakaan]]. Before the establishment of [[The Kingdom of Galifar\|The Kingdom]] but after [[Humans\|Humans]] arrived on the continent, many goblins were enslaved and made to build cities on top of the ruins of the [[Empire of Dhakaan\|Empire]]. This practice quickly ceased once slavery of all kinds was completed outlawed by [[Galifar I\|Galifar I]], leading to many [[Goblin\|Goblins]] integrating into the society of [[The Five Nations\|The Five Nations]].

Although still incredibly organized, goblin civilization is still only a shadow of its former self. Being natives, they are found all throughout the continent, but do have an organized nation in [[Darguun\|Darguun]].
