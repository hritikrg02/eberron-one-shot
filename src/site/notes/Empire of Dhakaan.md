The **Empire of Dhakaan** was an ancient [[Goblin\|Goblin]] empire that used to encompass the entirety of [[Khorvaire\|Khorvaire]]. The fall of the empire was due an invasion of the [[Daelkyr\|Daelkyr]] and their aberrant armies. As such, various ruins dot the landscape of [[Khorvaire\|Khorvaire]] containing treasures untold, causing many an adventurer to seek these ruins in hopes of striking it rich, much to the chagrin of the [[Goblin\|Goblin]] people.
