Deployed during [[The Last War\|The Last War]] as [[Aundair\|Aundair]]’s main military force, the **Knights Arcane** consist primarily of light cavalry units that engage in combat specializing with wands and staves. The leaders of the Knights Arcane have positions within the [[Government of Arcanix\|Government of Arcanix]].
