The **Mark of Making** is a [[Dragonmark\|Dragonmark]] that traditionally belongs to [[House Cannith\|House Cannith]] and grants an individual with it an exceptional ability to create all manner of things. Those with an even greater ability can even create magic-based items with ease. Many with this seal find themselves as artisans of weaponry, woodworking, alchemy, and more.
