The **Courier’s Guild** is operated and staffed by [[House Orien\|House Orien]]. It is responsible for the transportation of mundane objects throughout [[Khorvaire\|Khorvaire]] through its postal and mail service. They also operate a more secretive branch for the transportation of highly sensitive items.
