The **Mark of Shadow** is a [[Dragonmark\|Dragonmark]] that traditionally belongs to both [[House Phiarlan\|House Phiarlan]] and [[House Thuranni\|House Thuranni]] grants an individual with it abilities to create illusions, disguise oneself, and avoid detection. 

It is unique among [[Dragonmark\|Dragonmarks]] in that it is claimed by more than one of [[The Dragonmarked Houses\|The Dragonmarked Houses]].
