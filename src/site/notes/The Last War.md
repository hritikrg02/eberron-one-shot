**The Last War** was a civil war that recently took place on the continent of [[Khorvaire\|Khorvaire]]. It lasted from 894 YK - 996 YK. In the year 894 YK, [[Princess Mishann\|Princess Mishann]] was poised to take the throne and become the next ruler of [[The Kingdom of Galifar\|The Kingdom]]. However, conflict emerged between her and her siblings – each of whom (including herself) were in charge of one [[The Five Nations\|The Five Nations]]. As a result, the bloodiest conflict in all of [[Khorvaire\|Khorvaire]] commenced.

The only allied nations during the last war were [[Cyre\|Cyre]] and [[Aundair\|Aundair]]. [[Breland\|Breland]], [[Karrnath\|Karrnath]], and [[Thrane\|Thrane]] did not band together, instead the conflict was four-way, with these three nations and the alliance slaughtering each other for over a century. Surrounded on all sides and far from their single ally, [[Cyre\|Cyre]] resorted to hired mercenaries to act a militaristic force during the Last War: the [[Elf\|Elves]] hailing from the [[Cyre\|Cyre]] controlled region of [[Valenar\|Valenar]]. Due to their magical and engineering prowess, [[Cyre\|Cyre]] also created mechanical golems to fight within the war – beings now known as [[Warforged\|Warforged]]. It was the latter that allowed the [[Cyre\|Cyre]] to survive the vast majority of the war, as production of the [[Warforged\|Warforged]] only increased the war raged on. 

In 994 YK, exactly a century from when the war began, a magical cataclysm known as [[The Day of Mourning\|The Day of Mourning]] caused the nation of [[Cyre\|Cyre]] to become consumed. Since the cause of this event was unknown, leaders from all nations began to speak to one another. Fearful that the event could happen again, negotiations of a cease-fire immediately went underway. These talks culminated with [[The Treaty of Thronehold\|The Treaty of Thronehold]], which was signed by all parties in 996 YK and marked the end of the war as well as the [[The Kingdom of Galifar\|The Kingdom of Galifar]]. 

Although war as ceased for the time being, many believe this is only a temporary measure.
