> [!quote] Cutter, burglar and Kundarak excoriate
> My family has the finest vaults you can imagine. They forge the locks that secure royal jewels. And I learned to pick those those locks when I was barely out of the crib.

**House Kundarak** is one of [[The Dragonmarked Houses\|The Dragonmarked Houses]] and controls the [[Mark of Warding\|Mark of Warding]]. Using their abilities, they are master locksmiths. The vaults they secure are said to be unbreakable, with nary a thief being able to see what’s hidden inside. This ability to secure places and things lead to the creation of the [[Banking Guild\|Banking Guild]] and [[Warding Guild\|Warding Guild]].
