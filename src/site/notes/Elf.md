**Elves** in [[Eberron\|Eberron]] originate from the continent of [[Xen’drik\|Xen’drik]], wherein tens of thousands of years ago they were slaves to the [[Giants\|Giants]] that ruled over them. After much subjugation, the elven race rose up against the giants, a defining moment in their history known as the [[Elven Uprising\|Elven Uprising]]. Those elves that remained loyal to the giants would have their bodies infused with magic, forming the [[Drow\|Drow]]. This conflict culminated in elves fleeing [[Xen’drik\|Xen’drik]] to [[Aerenal\|Aerenal]], of which a some migrated to [[Khorvaire\|Khorvaire]] and other continents. 

They can be now found throughout [[Eberron\|Eberron]], including [[Khorvaire\|Khorvaire]] with several different cultures prevalent throughout. Within [[Khorvaire\|Khorvaire]], most elves have adapted to the culture of wherever they reside. The exception to this is [[Valenar\|Valenar]], a nation of exclusively elves extremely militaristic in nature.
