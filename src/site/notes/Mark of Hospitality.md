The **Mark of Hospitality** is a [[Dragonmark\|Dragonmark]] that traditionally belongs to [[House Ghallanda\|House Ghallanda]] and grants an individual with it abilities that are beneficial in service to others, such as cooking, cleaning, and other general measures of hospitality.
