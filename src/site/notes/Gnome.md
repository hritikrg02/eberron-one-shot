> [!quote] Zil proverb
> Five words can defeat a thousand swords.

**Gnomes** in [[Khorvaire\|Khorvaire]] can most commonly be found in the nation of [[Zilargo\|Zilargo]], but much like the other species of the continent, can be found throughout. They are not native to [[Khorvaire\|Khorvaire]], and when they first arrived many moons ago, they quickly realized that they would be any match for any of the much more physically capable species. From this, they quickly gathered as much information as possible – a trend that continues into the modern day. As such, gnomes are best known for their abilities in cunning, manipulation, and espionage, often relying on their words rather than a dagger. They often act as informants or spies. Many gnomes are also part of [[The Trust\|The Trust]].
