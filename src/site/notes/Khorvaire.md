The most populous of the continents of [[Eberron\|Eberron]], **Khorvaire** consists of one main landmass and several smaller islands to the northwest, east, and northeast. SeAs per the conditions of [[The Treaty of Thronehold\|The Treaty of Thronehold]], twelve different nations are recognized as sovereign states:
- [[Aundair\|Aundair]]
- [[Breland\|Breland]]
- [[Darguun\|Darguun]]
- The [[Eldeen Reaches\|Eldeen Reaches]]
- [[Karrnath\|Karrnath]]
- The [[Talenta Plains\|Talenta Plains]]
- [[Thrane\|Thrane]]
- [[Valenar\|Valenar]]
- [[Zilargo\|Zilargo]]

A set of [[Common Laws\|Common Laws]] are shared between all twelve nations and diplomatic relations are largely maintained, lest the continent be driven [[The Last War\|back to war]]. Besides the twelve nations, the [[Shadow Marches\|Shadow Marches]] and [[Demon Wastes\|Demon Wastes]] are unclaimed by any nation. Furthermore, the region of [[Droaam\|Droaam]] has declared itself as a nation, but not other nation yet acknowledges its sovereignty.

The remaining countries of [[The Five Nations\|The Five Nations]] hold the most power, being [[Aundair\|Aundair]], [[Breland\|Breland]], [[Karrnath\|Karrnath]], and [[Thrane\|Thrane]].
