> [!quote] The Draconic Prophecy
> When the gorgon’s breath gives life instead of stony death, when fires create instead of destroy, when towers rise from rubble and reach into the sky, then shall embers spring to life in the darkness and four shall rise to cause a dragon’s fall.

**The Draconic Prophecy** is the prophecy that applies to the entire world of [[Eberron\|Eberron]] as we know it. Elements of the prophecy are phrased as conditional statement – *if/when* something happens, *then* some outcome will occur. These statements can usually be interpreted in myriad ways making individual statements as well as the prophecy as a whole incredibly difficult to study, leading to only those who are immortal to dedicate themselves to studying the prophecy (such as [[Dragon Scholars\|Dragon Scholars]]). The statements do not describe a singular future, but rather the steps that must be taken in order for an outcome to occur. The scope of the statements are also inconsistent, with some being incredibly minor and others describing events that could completely change the world.

The prophecy itself is not one singular body of text, but appears throughout the world at seemingly random. Examples include the ways lava may flow down the mountain, the pattern made by the moons in the sky, or even on individuals as [[Dragonmark\|Dragonmarks]].
