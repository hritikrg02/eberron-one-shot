The **Mark of Detection** is a [[Dragonmark\|Dragonmark]] that traditionally belongs to [[House Medani\|House Medani]] and grants an individual with it to see that which cannot normally be seen or otherwise ascertained – from detecting poison, magic, or even evil intent. It originally manifested on [[Khoravar\|Khoravar]].
