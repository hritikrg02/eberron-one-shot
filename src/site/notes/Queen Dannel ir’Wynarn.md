**Queen Dannel ir’Wynarn** is the last officially recognized ruler of [[Cyre\|Cyre]]. She is presumed to have died in 996 YK, as she was in [[Metrol\|Metrol]] on [[The Day of Mourning\|The Day of Mourning]]. She is mother to [[Prince Oargev ir’Wynarn\|Prince Oargev ir’Wynarn]].
