> [!quote] From the [[Oath of Lyrandar\|Oath of Lyrandar]]
> Sovereigns and firstborn, grant me the four blessings promised to our people: dominion over the air, dominion over the water, fortune for my family, and fortune for my future.

**House Lyrandar** is one of [[The Dragonmarked Houses\|The Dragonmarked Houses]] and controls the [[Mark of Storm\|Mark of Storm]]. Using their abilities, this house has achieved dominance over all travel from the seas and, more recently, the skies. Moreover, this house completely controls all major farming operations, utilizing the weather to ensure good harvests for those who are willing to pay. To both of these ends, they run the [[Raincallers’ Guild\|Raincallers’ Guild]].
