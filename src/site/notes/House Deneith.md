> [!quote] Harric d’Deneith, [[Sentinel Marshals\|Sentinel Marshal]]
> Protection is my purpose. I defend the innocent from those who could do them harm. For my siblings, this is a job; for me, it’s a calling.

**House Deneith** is one of [[The Dragonmarked Houses\|The Dragonmarked Houses]] and controls the [[Mark of Sentinel\|Mark of Sentinel]]. They are well-known for their extremely defensive capabilities, providing exceptional bodyguard services to all those who are able to pay. They also operate the [[Blademarks Guild\|Blademarks Guild]], [[Defender’s Guild\|Defender’s Guild]], and [[Sentinel Marshals\|Sentinel Marshals]].
