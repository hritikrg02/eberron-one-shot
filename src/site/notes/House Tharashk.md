> [!quote] Hondar’Aashta, Tharashk bounty Hunter
> My ancestors used their gift to track beasts in the swamps. I use mine to find fugitives in the slums of [[Sharn, the City of Towers\|Sharn]]. I’m still a hunter. I always find my mark.

**House Tharashk** is one of [[The Dragonmarked Houses\|The Dragonmarked Houses]] and controls the [[Mark of Finding\|Mark of Finding]]. Members of this house excel at being detectives or bounty hunters. They operate the [[Finder’s Guild\|Finder’s Guild]].
