The **Sentinel Marshals** are a group staffed and operated by [[House Deneith\|House Deneith]]. They are granted the authority to pursue criminals and enforce the law across [[Khorvaire\|Khorvaire]]. They are known for their extreme honor – being a Marshal is considered a privilege, and with it comes high expectations.
