**Cyre** was one of [[The Five Nations\|The Five Nations]] within [[The Kingdom of Galifar\|The Kingdom of Galifar]]. Although once the crown-jewel of [[The Kingdom of Galifar\|The Kingdom]], the incident known as [[The Day of Mourning\|The Day of Mourning]] completed eradicated the nation as a whole, killing 2 in 3 of the inhabitants. The land that once was home to this prosperous nation is now referred to as [[The Mournlands\|The Mournlands]].

Before the war, Cyre was the seat of all rulers to [[The Kingdom of Galifar\|The Kingdom of Galifar]]. It was often referred to as *Beautiful Cyre* or *Wondrous Cyre*. It was the most powerful, most beautiful, and the richest nation of [[The Five Nations\|The Five Nations]]. [[Cyran\|Cyran]]s were known for their study and appreciation of philosophy, art, music, jewelry, magic, engineering, and more. It was commonplace to practice what would become known as [[Cyran Appreciation\|Cyran Appreciation]].

Although the nation itself only now exists in the hearts and minds of it’s refugees, there does exist a refugee camp lead by son of [[Queen Dannel ir’Wynarn\|Queen Dannel ir’Wynarn]], [[Prince Oargev ir’Wynarn\|Prince Oargev ir’Wynarn]] in the east of [[Breland\|Breland]], referred to as [[New Cyre\|New Cyre]].
