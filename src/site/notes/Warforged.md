Originally created by [[Cyre\|Cyre]] to fight in [[The Last War\|The Last War]], **Warforged** are mechanical lifeforms that have since lost their original purpose. [[The Treaty of Thronehold\|The Treaty of Thronehold]] reticently gave the warforged the right to become people, though hostility towards the warforged continue to this day. To many, they are a living reminder of [[The Last War\|The Last War]] and everything it took. Moreover, in the ever-industrializing world we find ourselves in, warforged are uniquely poised to work as they need neither food or sleep – representing a completely different threat than what they presented during the [[The Last War\|The Last War]] to many.

Some choose to live in [[The Mournlands\|The Mournlands]], technically their home nation, as they are unable to be killed by [[The Mist\|The Mist]]. But many choose to live among the other species of [[Eberron\|Eberron]], despite everything.
