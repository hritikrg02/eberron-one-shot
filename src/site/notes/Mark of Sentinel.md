The **Mark of Sentinel** is a [[Dragonmark\|Dragonmark]] that traditionally belongs to [[House Deneith\|House Deneith]] and grants an individual with it the protect others. It heightens the bearer’s senses, allowing them to respond to threats with uncanny speed.
