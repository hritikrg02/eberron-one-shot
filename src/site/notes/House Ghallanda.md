> [!quote] Alara d’Ghallanda, [[Halfling\|Halfling]] bartender
> Welcome, welcome! So good to see you again. Let me get your drink – blackroot tal with honey, yes? And then you have to listen to this story I’ve heard about the strange things going on in [[The Cogs\|The Cogs]].

**House Ghallanda** is one of [[The Dragonmarked Houses\|The Dragonmarked Houses]] and controls the [[Mark of Hospitality\|Mark of Hospitality]]. As such many of the inns, taverns, and restaurants across [[Khorvaire\|Khorvaire]] are operated either directly through the house, or licensed through the [[Hosteler’s Guild\|Hosteler’s Guild]]. Additionally, they run a franchise of inns known as the [[Gold Dragon Inn\|Gold Dragon Inn]].
