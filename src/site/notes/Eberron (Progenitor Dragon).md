**Eberron** was one of the three [[Progenitor Dragon\|Progenitor Dragon]]s who created [[Eberron\|Eberron]]. In fact, she eventually became the [[Eberron\|Material Plane]] itself, after she restrained [[Khyber (Progenitor Dragon)\|Khyber]] from taking over following the murder of [[Siberys (Progenitor Dragon)\|Siberys]].
