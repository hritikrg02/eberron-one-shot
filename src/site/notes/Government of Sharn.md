The **Government of Sharn** has all manners of positions of power such as beadles, ministers, and bailiffs among others but can broadly be described as a *parliamentary representative democracy*. Citizens of the [[Wards of Sharn\|Wards of Sharn]] plus that of [[The Cogs\|The Cogs]] and [[The Skyway\|The Skyway]] each vote on a representative to be on the [[City Council of Sharn\|City Council]], though the exact methodology used differs from ward to ward.
