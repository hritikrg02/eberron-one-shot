The **Church of the Silver Flame** is one the main [[Religions in Khorvaire\|Religions in Khorvaire]] and mostly closely associated with the nation of [[Thrane\|Thrane]] though followers do exist in other nations (primarily [[Breland\|Breland]] and [[Aundair\|Aundair]]). Its main teachings include practicing charity, austerity, and equality – all of which come from [[Tira Miron\|Tira Miron]], a paladin who sacrificed herself to slay demons and protect others.
