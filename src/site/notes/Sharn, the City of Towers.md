Located in the nation of [[Breland\|Breland]] on [[The Hilt\|The Hilt]], **Sharn** is the largest city in all of [[Eberron\|Eberron]] and is considered the first true metropolis of the modern age. It is the industrial and invention capital of the world, where most of the technology relied upon by other nations originate, both in ideation and creation. It’s home to the [[Lightning Rail\|Lightning Rail]].

The city of Sharn is built upon the ruins of an even grander and larger city once a part of the ancient [[Empire of Dhakaan\|Empire of Dhakaan]].
