 Established in the year 1 YK by [[King Galifar\|King Galifar]], **The Kingdom of Galifar** the kingdom that laid claim to the continent of [[Khorvaire\|Khorvaire]] for almost nine centuries. The kingdom was quite prosperous and consisted of the [[The Five Nations\|The Five Nations]], wherein each was led was a member of the royal family following the passing of [[King Galifar\|King Galifar]]. The leader of [[Cyre\|Cyre]] was considered the bloodline that would continue to rule the Kingdom. The Kingdom was officially dismantled during the signing of [[The Treaty of Thronehold\|The Treaty of Thronehold]], though many say it died on [[The Day of Mourning\|The Day of Mourning]].
