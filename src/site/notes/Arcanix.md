**Arcanix** is a floating city in [[Aundair\|Aundair]] best known for being the magical research capital of [[Eberron\|Eberron]]. The city itself mainly consists of several universities (including [[The Arcane Congress\|The Arcane Congress]]), where magic is studied at every hour of every day, and students and scholars alike bicker endlessly about all things wizardry. It is technically a monarchy (see [[Government of Arcanix\|Government of Arcanix]]).
