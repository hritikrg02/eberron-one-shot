The **Mark of Healing** is a [[Dragonmark\|Dragonmark]] that traditionally belongs to [[House Jorasco\|House Jorasco]] and grants an individual with it the ability to cure disease and injury. It also helps with understanding of the malady in question, aiding in finding a cure.
