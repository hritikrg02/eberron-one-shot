> [!quote]  Dalin d’Vadalis
> Don’t get sentimental. A beast is a tool. Our role is to find the right tool for the task and to make sure they’re of the finest quality. Nature is out kingdom: never forget that we were born to rule it.

**House Vadalis** is one of [[The Dragonmarked Houses\|The Dragonmarked Houses]] and controls the [[Mark of Handling\|Mark of Handling]]. Using this ability, the house operates hundreds of stables, ranches, and other enclosures for all manner of beast. They control an immense amount of the livestock of [[Eberron\|Eberron]] and also engage in [[Magebreeding\|Magebreeding]], creating a plethora of enhanced creatures as well as [[Chimera\|Chimera]].
