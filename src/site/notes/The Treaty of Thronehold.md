Signed in 996 YK, **The Treaty of Thronehold** was the document that ended [[The Last War\|The Last War]] and established the current state of nations in [[Khorvaire\|Khorvaire]], completely dismantling the monarchy of [[The Kingdom of Galifar\|The Kingdom of Galifar]]. As a result, no victor to the war was declared. The signage occurred a mere two years after [[The Day of Mourning\|The Day of Mourning]], with negotiations happening immediately after the incident.
