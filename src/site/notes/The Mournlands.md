**The Mournlands** are all that remain of [[Cyre\|Cyre]] after [[The Day of Mourning\|The Day of Mourning]]. An eradiated wasteland, the air is filled with a mist that mutates and destroys anyone or anything that it comes in contact with. Although considered a nation by [[The Treaty of Thronehold\|The Treaty of Thronehold]], that is name only. No ruler lays claim to the land, and no known populous resides therein.
