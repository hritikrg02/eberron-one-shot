**Cyran Appreciation** is a cultural movement about slowing down and appreciating what is around oneself, such as nature and art. It is named as such due to its prevalence within [[Cyre\|Cyre]]. Although some [[Cyran Refugees\|Cyran Refugees]] still practice this ideology, it has widely fallen out of fashion as ideas of rapid progress and innovation are more widely held.
