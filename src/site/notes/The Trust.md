**The Trust** is a secret police organization within [[Khorvaire\|Khorvaire]] staffed exclusively by [[Gnome\|Gnome]]s, and is a network of spies and assassins. It is a part of the [[Government of Zilargo\|Government of Zilargo]]. The goal of The Trust is to eliminate any and all threats to society.  Its reach stretches to every corner of the continent. Nobody, not even other [[Gnome\|Gnomes]], know who is part of the trust, leading to a general distrust of [[Gnome\|Gnomes]] overall. It is believed that the amount of information held by The Trust is vast and grows by the day.
