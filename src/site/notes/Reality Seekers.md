**Reality Seekers** refer to a cultural group within [[Changling\|Changling]] society defined by their lack of shifting out of their base [[Changling\|Changling]] form. To them, using another form is simply for the comfort of others and they instead wish to be accepted for who they are as [[Changling\|Changling]]s, appearance and all.
