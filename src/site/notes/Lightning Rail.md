The **Lighting Rail** is a network of trains that connect all of [[Khorvaire\|Khorvaire]]. It is relied upon by all nations in [[Khorvaire\|Khorvaire]] for easy and fast transportation with the [[Lightning Rail Main Hub\|main hub]] being located in [[Sharn, the City of Towers\|Sharn]]. The organization and networking of the lightning rail is run by [[House Orien\|House Orien]].
