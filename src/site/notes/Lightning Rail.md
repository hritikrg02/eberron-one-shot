The **Lighting Rail** is a network of trains that connect all of [[Khorvaire\|Khorvaire]]. It is relied upon by all nations in [[Khorvaire\|Khorvaire]] for easy and fast transportation. The [[Lightning Rail Main Hub\|main hub]] is located in [[Sharn, the City of Towers\|Sharn]].
