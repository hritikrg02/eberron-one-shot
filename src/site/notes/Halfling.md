> [!quote] Sir Danton ir’Lain, Brelish Knight
> Zombies? Minotaurs? They don’t scare me a bit. But a howling halfling warrior charging on a clawfoot raptor? Most terrifying thing I’ve every seen. 

It is quite common to see **halflings** fully integrated into human society within the former [[The Five Nations\|Five Nations]]. In fact, halfling society funded a series of inns that span the entirety of [[Khorvaire\|Khorvaire]] with some even being found throughout [[Eberron\|Eberron]] as a whole. 

Otherwise, they are found in the nation of [[Talenta Plains\|Talenta Plains]], having a nomadic lifestyle. Despite being nomadic tribes, the settlements that do exist are often more metropolitan that one may exist. These often number in the thousands and their mount of choice are various forms of dinosaur.
