The **City Council of Sharn** is a body of seventeen elected representatives: one for each [[Wards of Sharn\|Ward of Sharn]]plus the [[The Cogs\|The Cogs]] and [[The Skyway\|The Skyway]]. They currently consist of the following members:
- [[Sorik Sensos\|Sorik Sensos]] representing [[Middle Central\|Middle Central]],
- [[Sava Kharisa\|Sava Kharisa]] representing [[Lower Central\|Lower Central]],
- [[Thurik Davandi\|Thurik Davandi]] representing [[Upper Menthis\|Upper Menthis]],
- [[Savia Potellas\|Savia Potellas]] representing [[Lower Menthis\|Lower Menthis]],
- [[Maza Thadian\|Maza Thadian]] representing [[Upper Northedge\|Upper Northedge]],
- [[Shassa Tarr\|Shassa Tarr]] representing [[Lower Northedge\|Lower Northedge]],
- [[Bestian ir’Tonn\|Bestian ir’Tonn]] representing [[Upper Tavick’s Landing\|Upper Tavick’s Landing]],
- [[Kilk\|Kilk]] representing [[Lower Tavick’s Landing\|Lower Tavick’s Landing]],
- [[Hruitt\|Hruitt]] representing [[Middle Dura\|Middle Dura]],
- [[Ilyra Boromar\|Ilyra Boromar]] representing [[Lower Dura\|Lower Dura]],
- [[Evix ir’Marasha\|Evix ir’Marasha]] representing [[The Skyway\|The Skyway]], and
- [[Nolan Toranak\|Nolan Toranak]] representing [[The Cogs\|The Cogs]].
