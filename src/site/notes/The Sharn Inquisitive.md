**The Sharn Inquisitive** is one of the most popular news publications in [[Sharn, the City of Towers\|Sharn]] and can be found in almost anywhere news is sold across [[Khorvaire\|Khorvaire]]. They are considered reputable and relatively neutral, with a slight bias toward their home nation of [[Breland\|Breland]].
