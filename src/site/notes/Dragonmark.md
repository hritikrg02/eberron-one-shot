**Dragonmarks** are tattoo like symbols that appear on mortal individuals connected to [[The Draconic Prophecy\|The Draconic Prophecy]]. Since They are passed down family lines though can also appear at random on individuals at any point during their life. Depending on the mark present, people who possess them are able to wield special abilities.

Twelve established dragonmarks are known to currently exist:
- The [[Mark of Detection\|Mark of Detection]]
- The [[Mark of Finding\|Mark of Finding]]
- The [[Mark of Handling\|Mark of Handling]]
- The [[Mark of Healing\|Mark of Healing]]
- The [[Mark of Hospitality\|Mark of Hospitality]]
- The [[Mark of Making\|Mark of Making]]
- The [[Mark of Passage\|Mark of Passage]]
- The [[Mark of Scribing\|Mark of Scribing]]
- The [[Mark of Sentinel\|Mark of Sentinel]]
- The [[Mark of Shadow\|Mark of Shadow]]
- The [[Mark of Storm\|Mark of Storm]]
- The [[Mark of Warding\|Mark of Warding]]

Each of these are tied to one or more of [[The Dragonmarked Houses\|The Dragonmarked Houses]] (see [[Dragonmark Lookup Table\|Dragonmark Lookup Table]]). Additionally, there is a thirteenth mark that has been known to manifest but is not associated with any house: the  [[Aberrant Dragonmark\|Aberrant Dragonmark]]. Each of the above dragonmarks can manifest as a [[Minor Dragonmark\|Minor Dragonmark]], [[Greater Dragonmark\|Greater Dragonmark]], or [[Siberys Dragonmark\|Siberys Dragonmark]]. Much like elements of the [[The Draconic Prophecy\|The Draconic Prophecy]], simply being dragonmarked does not indicate with what scope an individual will affect the world.
