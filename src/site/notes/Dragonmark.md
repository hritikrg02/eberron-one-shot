**Dragonmarks** are tattoo like symbols that appear on mortal individuals connected to [[The Draconic Prophecy\|The Draconic Prophecy]]. Since They are passed down family lines though can also appear at random on individuals at any point during their life. Depending on the mark present, people who possess them are able to wield special abilities.

Twelve established dragonmarks are known to currently exist:
- The [[Dragonmark of Detection\|Dragonmark of Detection]]
- The [[Dragonmark of Finding\|Dragonmark of Finding]]
- The [[Dragonmark of Handling\|Dragonmark of Handling]]
- The [[Dragonmark of Healing\|Dragonmark of Healing]]
- The [[Dragonmark of Hospitality\|Dragonmark of Hospitality]]
- The [[Dragonmark of Making\|Dragonmark of Making]]
- The [[Dragonmark of Passage\|Dragonmark of Passage]]
- The [[Dragonmark of Scribing\|Dragonmark of Scribing]]
- The [[Dragonmark of Sentinel\|Dragonmark of Sentinel]]
- The [[Dragonmark of Shadow\|Dragonmark of Shadow]]
- The [[Dragonmark of Storm\|Dragonmark of Storm]]
- The [[Dragonmark of Warding\|Dragonmark of Warding]]

Each of these are tied to one or more of [[The Dragonmarked Houses\|The Dragonmarked Houses]]. Additionally, there is a thirteenth mark that has been known to manifest but is not associated with any house: the  [[Aberrant Dragonmark\|Aberrant Dragonmark]].

Each of the above dragonmarks can manifest as a [[Standard Dragonmark\|Standard Dragonmark]], [[Greater Dragonmark\|Greater Dragonmark]], or [[Siberys Dragonmark\|Siberys Dragonmark]].
