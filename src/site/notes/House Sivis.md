> [!quote] Barron Lysse Lyrriman d’Sivis
> Communication is the foundation of civilization.

**House Sivis** is one of [[The Dragonmarked Houses\|The Dragonmarked Houses]] and controls the [[Mark of Scribing\|Mark of Scribing]]. Members of this house specialize in both the spoken word (e.g. translators and mediators) and the written word (e.g. notaries). This, when paired with [[Gnome\|Gnomes]]’ general ability to seek and retain information, makes this house a force to be reckoned with, allowing clientele to retrieve almost any information they seek.
