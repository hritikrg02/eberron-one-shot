**The Five Nations** refer to the five primary regions that we under the [[The Kingdom of Galifar\|The Kingdom of Galifar]]:
- [[Aundair\|Aundair]],
- [[Breland\|Breland]],
- [[Cyre\|Cyre]],
- [[Karrnath\|Karrnath]], and
- [[Thrane\|Thrane]].

Together, these nations laid claim to most of the land of [[Khorvaire\|Khorvaire]]. However once [[The Kingdom of Galifar\|The Kingdom]] was dismantled through [[The Treaty of Thronehold\|The Treaty of Thronehold]], the offciality of the Five Nations no longer existed on paper. That being said, the remnants of the Five Nations (being all of the above sans [[Cyre\|Cyre]], becoming the [[The Mournlands\|The Mournlands]]) do still hold by far the most amount of power, and as such the name *The Five Nations* continues to be used to refer to this grouping.
