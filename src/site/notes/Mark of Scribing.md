The **Mark of Scribing** is a [[Dragonmark\|Dragonmark]] that traditionally belongs to [[House Sivis\|House Sivis]] and grants an individual with it an exceptional ability to communicate. This communication has the potential to transcend language, species, and even extreme distance.
